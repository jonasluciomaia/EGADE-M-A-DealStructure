Interpretation

What the current structure is doing

    Model scope and simplifications
    • Share counts are user-provided and are not automatically adjusted to fully diluted shares using the treasury stock method.
    • The model values the equity purchase price only. Target net debt is not automatically refinanced at the acquirer’s Kd.
    • No purchase price allocation, asset step-up, new intangible amortization, or goodwill impairment is modeled.
    • Synergies are assumed to be recurring and fully realized in year one. Integration costs and phase-in are not modeled.
    • Available cash is used before new debt, with no minimum operating-cash balance.
    • Transaction costs are funded with cash and debt but excluded from recurring EPS. Their financing cost remains in recurring interest expense.
    • For mixed consideration, the offer is stated as cash per target share plus the stock exchange ratio.
