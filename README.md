
## Version 6 calculation corrections

- Lost after-tax income on cash used is subtracted from recurring pro-forma net income.
- Transaction costs are additional uses of funds, financed first with available cash and then new debt.
- Transaction costs are excluded from recurring EPS, but interest on debt raised to fund them remains in recurring EPS.
- Firepower is tested against total uses: equity purchase price plus transaction costs.
- Weighted consideration financing-cost weights use total uses as the denominator.
- EPS interpretation compares the acquirer's earnings yield with after-tax Kd rather than assuming cash is always less dilutive.
- Ke remains an opportunity-cost input and does not directly enter accounting EPS.

## Model scope

Share counts are user-provided rather than automatically fully diluted under the treasury stock method. Target net debt is not refinanced. Purchase price allocation, asset step-ups, acquired-intangible amortization, goodwill impairment, integration costs, and synergy phase-in are outside scope. Synergies are fully realized in year one. Available cash is used before debt with no minimum operating-cash balance.
